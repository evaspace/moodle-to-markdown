# Composée d'injections, de surjections

- $f$, $g$ injectives $\Rightarrow g \circ f$ injective ; $f$, $g$ surjectives $\Rightarrow g \circ f$ surjective.
- $g \circ f$ injective $\Rightarrow f$ injective ; $g \circ f$ surjective $\Rightarrow g$ surjective.
- $f$, $g$ bijectives $\Rightarrow (g \circ f)^{-1} = f^{-1} \circ g^{-1}$.
